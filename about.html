Our Story

Started small, built to scale — for our clients and ourselves

Mani Corps began as a two-person studio building WordPress sites for local businesses that couldn't get a straight answer — or a straight invoice — from bigger agencies. That frustration became our founding principle: transparent scope, honest timelines, and work that holds up under real traffic.

Today we're a full digital agency serving startups, corporates, e-commerce brands, and educational institutions across multiple countries, but the original promise hasn't changed — every project gets senior attention, not a template.

We've since expanded from websites into ERP, CRM, and full-funnel marketing, because our clients kept asking us to solve the next problem after launch, not just the first one.

Where is Mani Corps based?

We're headquartered in Karachi, Pakistan, and serve clients internationally through remote-first workflows.

How many people work on my project?

Every project is assigned a small senior team — typically a designer, developer, and project lead — rather than a large rotating group.

Do you sign NDAs or contracts?

Yes, every engagement starts with a signed scope of work, and an NDA is available on request.
