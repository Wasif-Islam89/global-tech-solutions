Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does a typical project take?

Most business websites launch within 3–5 weeks. ERP and CRM projects typically run 6–10 weeks depending on scope.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance after launch?

Yes. Every project includes a maintenance option covering updates, backups, security monitoring, and content changes.

Can you work with international clients?

Absolutely. We work with clients across time zones using structured async updates and scheduled video calls.

What platforms do you build on?

We build custom-coded sites (HTML/CSS/JS), WordPress, and connect them to bespoke ERP/CRM backends as needed.
